## Design Rationale (Based on Competitor Research)

The competitor analysis reveals five critical UX gaps that AquaPlan addresses:

1. **Fragmentation → Unified AI-first experience.** AqAdvisor does compatibility, Tropica does plants, Aquarium Co-Op does content — none combines planning + AI + image recognition in one app. Our single-flow onboarding wizard eliminates context-switching.

2. **Expert-only → Beginner-first with progressive disclosure.** 2Hr Aquarist and Practical Fishkeeping cater to intermediate+ hobbyists. Our Onboarding Wizard defaults to "easy" recommendations and explains the "why" behind every suggestion — matching Tropica's proven difficulty-tiered approach.

3. **Paludarium is completely unserved.** Zero commercial apps or tools exist for paludarium builders. Our Tank Type selector elevates paludarium to equal footing with traditional aquariums, with dedicated compatibility rules, humidity fields, and water/land ratio inputs.

4. **"Help me replicate this tank" has no tool.** The Image→Plan flow (camera → AI analysis → identified materials → generate plan) reduces a days-long Reddit/forum research cycle to seconds — our strongest differentiator.

5. **Material reuse = trust.** No competitor asks "what do you already have?" Our Step 4 signals "we're here to help, not upsell" — directly addressing the beginner's #1 budget concern.
